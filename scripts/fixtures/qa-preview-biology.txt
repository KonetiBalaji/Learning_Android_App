Chapter 1 Cells
Cells are the basic units of life in living organisms. Mitochondria release usable energy for cellular work. Ribosomes support protein synthesis inside cells. Cell membranes regulate what enters and leaves the cell. The nucleus contains genetic information that guides cellular activities.

Chapter 2 Plants
Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy in plants. Roots absorb water and minerals from the soil. Stems support leaves and transport materials throughout the plant. Leaves exchange gases through small openings called stomata. Flowers contain reproductive structures in flowering plants. Seeds contain an embryo and stored food for early growth. Germination begins when a seed takes up water under suitable conditions. Chlorophyll gives many leaves their green appearance. Xylem transports water from roots toward the leaves. Phloem carries sugars from leaves to other parts of a plant.
