# Science chapter explorers

All four Grade 8 Science catalog routes now have bounded native interaction. Cells
retains its separately documented organelle diagram. Matter selects solid, liquid or
gas particle spacing and motion; Force and Motion calculates distance for three
constant-speed examples; Ecosystems traces a clearly labelled, simplified 10% energy
transfer example from producer through tertiary consumer. Each control selects fixed,
trusted app content, exposes concise accessibility text and cannot submit an answer
or change mastery.

JVM tests verify every route, distinct matter descriptions, exact speed/time/distance
products, invalid-motion rejection and every 10% energy transition. Android JVM tests,
lint and debug assembly pass. The three new explorers do not yet have rendered-device,
TalkBack, physical-device or UAT evidence, and these examples do not establish broad
Science curriculum coverage or board alignment.

The fixed action/content design follows OA-03 in `openmaic-provenance.md`. No generated
action, HTML, script or OpenMAIC source code is executed or copied.
