# Open OER Grade 8 Foundation

Status: user-approved launch scope; implementation and automated QA remain subject
to the release ledger and user acceptance.

On 2026-09-07 the product owner authorized independently selected open educational
content without further board approval and retained Grade 8 as the initial launch
boundary. The product therefore identifies its current catalog as
`open-oer-foundation-2026-09`, not CBSE, ICSE, a state board, or another authority.

The 17 current topics and their deterministic teaching material are
application-authored. They are released under CC BY 4.0 in
`CURRICULUM-LICENSE.md`. No external textbook prose, questions, images, or answer
keys are packaged. In particular, NCERT's official terms prohibit redistribution
of its textbooks in software, so NCERT material is excluded rather than described
as open source.

Openly licensed resources may inform future educator review, but each imported or
adapted item must record its exact source, revision, license and attribution before
entering the application. A URL being publicly readable is not sufficient.

The scope approval resolves the catalog identity and permission boundary. It does
not prove educator review, age calibration, accessibility, live-model accuracy, or
physical-device UAT, and it does not expand the initial release beyond Grade 8.
